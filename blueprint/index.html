What actually happens

You download four files
and ask one question

The files belong to a cafe that does not exist. Eight weeks of takings, its bookings, its unanswered messages, its unpaid bills. All invented, so you can pull it apart without worrying about anybody's privacy, including your own.

  1. Download the folder. Four ordinary spreadsheet files, the kind almost every business already has somewhere.
  2. Point your assistant at it. No account to create. Nothing of yours connected to anything.
  3. Ask it a question in plain English. Which day of the week actually makes the money, and how much more than the quietest day.
  4. Read the answer. You did not open a file, sort a column, or write a formula. You did not know, and now you do.

It is a fast assistant, not an automation. Close the laptop and nothing happens tomorrow.

Nobody starts flight training with ground school. They book a trial lesson, go up, and hold the controls for a minute. You do not learn much in that minute. What happens is that the whole thing stops being an idea.

The version that works while you are asleep is what the book builds.

Who this is not for

Not for you if

You already build this sort of thing. You will find it slow, and you would be right to.

Probably for you if

You run something, you have heard about all this for two years, and you have never once had software do a job while you were asleep.

There is a lot written about this. Nearly all the good, detailed, generous material is written by people who build software, for other people who build software. The rest is a hook, a promise, and a link to a course.

Nobody stands in the middle. That is the entire reason this exists.
